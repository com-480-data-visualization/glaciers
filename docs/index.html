COM-480: Swiss Glaciers

Swiss Glaciers Evolution

Explore the history of Switzerland's changing icy landscapes. This project visualizes glacier retreat across the country, prioritizing dynamic change rates over static outlines. The interface allows for a transition from a broad national overview to glacier-specific data.
Precise evolutionary histories are highlighted for three iconic landmarks - the Aletschgletscher, Rhonegletscher, and Gornergletscher - demonstrating the scale of their transformation over time.

Aletschgletscher

Explore the largest glacier in the Alps and its historical retreat.

Rhonegletscher

Discover the source of the Rhone River and its protective ice blankets.

Gornergletscher

Dive into the data of the massive ice system near the Matterhorn.
