The second-largest and third-longest glacier system in the Alps, located near Zermatt on the west side of the Monte Rosa massif. It forms a complex system with several tributary glaciers converging in a spectacular ice landscape.
Monte Rosa Second largest Third longest Valley glacier Zermatt
💡

Fun Fact

A notable feature of this glacier is the Gornersee, an ice marginal lake at the confluence area of the Gorner Glacier and Grenzgletscher. This lake fills every year and drains in summer, usually as a glacial lake outburst flood. This is one of only a few glacial lakes in the Alps exhibiting this behavior.
