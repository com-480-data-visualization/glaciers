The Rhonegletscher located in the Swiss Alps, is the source of the Rhône river and one of the main feeders of Leman lake.
Easily accessible from the Furka Pass road, it has long been a popular tourist destination, and its ice grotto, carved directly into the glacier, has drawn visitors for over a century. Since 1850, however, the glacier has retreated dramatically, losing nearly 60% of its volume. To slow the melt, it is now partially covered with UV-resistant fleece blankets.

Fun Fact

The glacier was the subject of a poem by Victor Hugo, Dicté en présence du glacier du Rhône, in the collection Les Feuilles d'automne (Autumn Leaves).
